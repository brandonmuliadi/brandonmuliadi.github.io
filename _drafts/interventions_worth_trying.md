Problem: Some people (like me) are prone to chapped lips, and feel like they have to apply chapstick religiously.
Intervention worth trying: Before sleeping, wet your lips slightly, then apply petrolatum jelly on top. Also try variants like using lotion then petrolatum jelly.

Problem: Not feeling sleepy when you should.
Intervention worth trying: ~0.5mg melatonin, five hours before your usual bedtime.
